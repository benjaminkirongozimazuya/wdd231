NP Membership Details

This level is completely free and reserved for non-profit organizations operating in the Kolwezi region. It allows participation in general meetings and local community networks.

Bronze Membership Details

The Bronze level offers preferential rates for basic business workshops as well as a standard listing in our public online directory.

Silver Membership Details

The Silver level includes priority access to seminars, significant discounts on annual events, and moderate promotion of your professional services.

Gold Membership Details

The Gold level represents top-tier partnership. It includes a high-profile spotlight position on the Chamber home page, personalized support, and VIP access to all economic summits.
